Scriberman

Private, on-device meeting transcription for macOS.

  • Records microphone and system audio into per-session files.
  • Live, on-device speech-to-text with speaker diarization.
  • Re-run higher-quality offline transcription anytime.
  • Speaker memory, system-wide voice dictation, transcript tools.
  • No audio ever leaves your Mac for transcription.
